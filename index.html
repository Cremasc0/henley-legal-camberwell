05 / Costs and approach

Plain advice and clear costs.

Quality legal work at a reasonable cost. How we charge is set out before any advice is given.

Cost and benefit assessed before we act.

Before we take any step or give any advice, we undertake a cost and benefit assessment for the client, so the value of the work is weighed against what it will cost.

Hourly rates below non-specialist firms.

Our hourly rates at this specialist level are much lower than many non-specialist lawyers. That cost effectiveness comes from legal experience, efficiency and conscientiousness in delivery.

Volume discounts up to 20%.

We discount our hourly rates for clients with a high volume of legal work on an ongoing basis. Discounts can be as high as 20% for companies with an in-house legal department.

Promotional pricing on certain matters.

On some matters we offer promotional pricing or special arrangements for payment of our fees. Contact the office for the arrangements currently available.

Regular reporting, and reachable after hours.

We report regularly to clients on the progress of matters we handle, and our staff are accessible both during and after office hours. Each client's needs are different.

Documents handled electronically.

Documents and faxes are scanned, sorted and stored as soft copies, then forwarded by email, usually immediately. Instructions are acted on as soon as they are received, which reduces cost.
